I'm currently living in the mountains, near the ski areas, and regularly enjoying all of the wonderful freedoms that I have. Lately, my time has been spent traveling and seeing the world. I also own an Airstream and spend plenty of time in the woods, mountains, near the lakes, hot springs, and national parks, or pretty much wherever I want to park it for a while. I've continued to improve the trailer and enjoy more and more time camping off-grid and away from the busy cities.

For technology side projects, I'm just finishing up a project to convert my long running Debian server over to a TalosOS Kubernetes deployment. This box runs a small portion of my electronic life, making steady progress towards open source, self hosting, and data sovereignty. I knew K8s was going to be something amazing the first time I started using it, and after many professional years managing clusters, I'm happy to have this project running on my home computer.

In other news, I've recently adopted a couple of stray kittens from my property manager. My adult cat is not too thrilled, but I'm sure he will come around. Yes, they are adorable. And yes, I can't wait to get a full night sleep again.

Soon enough winter will be here and I'm looking forward to my regular downhill activities. As someone who has skied for over 40 years now, this is still one of my favorite things to do in life. While there's no snow on the ground, paddle boarding, swimming, mountain biking, and summer concerts have been a reasonable replacement. My recent Amtrak trip to Denver for a Red Rocks concert was amazing, I love the train and I love that venue. That trip gave me plenty of time to catch up on my reading too, and I continue to be enriched by Terry Pratchett's wonderful sense of humor and wit.
