In 2026, I've had a fun time juggling growing Masterpoint, working on athletic pursuits, and raising a puppy.

The puppy is the most important obviously, so I'll start there 😁 In January, I adopted a 6 month old rescue and named her Pingora. She's super loving, wicked smart, and an all-around amazing dog. Check out [my LinkedIn feed](https://www.linkedin.com/in/gowiem/) for the occasional photo.

Juggling Masterpoint has been fun as we've doubled our headcount in terms of full-time staff in the past six months, and we've added our first non-billable team member, both of which are milestones that I'm excited about. We're growing an incredible team of people that I deeply enjoy working with, our clients are very happy with our work, and I'm finding a lot of fulfillment in working to build a sustainable organization that gives back.

We're continuing to grow as well... so if you're reading this and you're interested in a position, please check out our [Careers page](/careers/) 😉

On the personal side, this year has been a big deviation from my standard climb-as-much-as-I-can thought process. I started out the year with a goal to run a 50 mile race. My early months of the year from January to April involved 100+ miles of training per month, culminating in me running the Rock the Ridge ultramarathon on May 2.

The other thing I put my efforts toward this year was working on doing [the LA Freeway](https://www.mountainproject.com/route/117783697/la-freeway-longs-to-arapaho-traverse) (Longs to Arapaho Traverse). The Freeway is a 38 mile, 20,000 feet of elevation gain technical ridge scramble starting in Rocky Mountain National Park and ending at the end of the Indian Peaks Wilderness. I put in many days of recon missions over the summer and one unsuccessful attempt in August before realizing this was going to be a multi-year objective, so check in next year to see if this objective gets ticked off!
