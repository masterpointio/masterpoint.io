We're an Infrastructure as Code consultancy, and we're hiring a **Head of Engineering** to lead our engineering team and own technical relationships with our clients. Today both of those jobs sit with our founder. Your job is to take them over, do them better than he can, and free him up to focus on growing the business.

This role is **part of Masterpoint's leadership team**. You'll work with the founder and Head of Delivery to set the direction and goals of the business and support execution.

This is a **player-coach role**. About half your time is management and client leadership. The other half you spend as a hands-on engineer or tech lead on client work, because that is how you learn how Masterpoint implements and how you keep the team's respect.

**This role is based in the Colorado Front Range.** We want the leadership team to spend real time together in person, so we're hiring this role in the Denver Metro. We aim to start a flexible hybrid schedule in 2027.

We're a team of 11 spread across North America and Ukraine. We value openness, work/life balance, and giving back to the communities we rely on. We operate more like a startup than a traditional consultancy: fast, high-trust, and quality-first.

## What You'll Do

### Lead the engineering team (primary)

- **Manage our engineers directly:** regular 1-1s, written growth plans, and direct feedback. You own engineering performance. When someone is off track, you name it early, set a written expectation with a date, and see it through. Our Head of Delivery owns People / HR process, and works these cases with you where needed.
- Own staffing and utilization across projects with our Head of Delivery: who is on what, when they roll off, and where the risk is.
- **Set and hold the technical bar:** architecture reviews, plan and PR reviews, and the standards for how Masterpoint implements inside client environments.
- Run the engineering side of our hiring process: technical interviews, scorecards, and the recommendation on who we bring on.
- **Coach by asking.** Several of our engineers are moving from senior toward staff-level work. You have made that move yourself, and you help them reach the answer on their own instead of handing it to them.

### Own client technical relationships (primary)

- **Be the technical point of contact for our clients' engineering leaders** (VPs of Engineering, Directors of DevOps, CTOs). Today that is the founder. Within six months a portion of our clients will have you as one of their primary contacts.
- Own technical escalations. When something goes sideways on a client platform, you pick it up, work it with the team, and communicate it clearly.
- Scope and estimate the technical side of proposals with our Head of Delivery and the founder, and gut-check scope with the team before it goes to a client.
- Spot expansion opportunities inside existing accounts and bring them to the founder.

### Stay hands-on (about 50%)

- Spend roughly half your time as a **billable engineer or tech lead** on one or two client projects.
- Same stack as the team: Terraform and OpenTofu, Cloud Engineering, GitHub Actions and Spacelift, and AI-assisted workflows.
- As the team grows this share shrinks. Expect it to stay meaningful through your first year.

### Shape how we build (ongoing)

- Steer our internal **AI IaC Harness**: the shared context, skills, and standards our engineers use with Claude Code and similar tools across very different client environments.
- Pick what works, make it the team standard, and write it down. A new tool earns its place by beating the current standard and not by just being new.

## What You Bring

### Must-Haves

- **Colorado based.** Applicants must currently live in the Colorado Front Range as we plan to have a hybrid office schedule in the Denver or Boulder area. Masterpoint does not sponsor applicants for work visas for this position.
- **3+ years managing engineers directly:** 1-1s, growth plans, performance, and hiring. You have taken someone through a performance plan or out of the team. You act quickly when there are performance problems and don't let issues linger.
- **Client-facing experience** in consulting, professional services, or a similar stakeholder-heavy role.
- 7+ years in the software industry with **real hands-on depth in IaC** (Terraform or OpenTofu) and at least one major cloud. All clouds are in top demand for us. You still ship code.
- **Current on AI-assisted engineering.** You use Claude Code, Cursor, or similar every day, and you have a point of view on how a team should adopt it well.
- Excellent written communication. Most of our client and team communication happens in writing.
- High ownership and agency. You spot the problem early and drive it to a resolution without being asked. You close loops: meetings end with an owner and a date, and you do what you said you would do.
- Experience with SOWs, estimation, and scope management in a services business.

### Nice-to-Haves

- You have run engineering at a boutique consultancy or agency of 5 to 20 people.
- Player-coach experience: you have managed a team while carrying a billable or delivery load yourself.
- IaC orchestration (Spacelift, Atlantis, Terraform Cloud, Scalr), policy as code (OPA and Rego), or container orchestration (Kubernetes, ECS).
- You have coached senior engineers into staff-level roles.
- Public writing or speaking in the infrastructure space.

## How You'll Be Measured

Some examples of how you'll be measured:

- Founder time on engineering management and technical client escalations drops by at least half within six months.
- Every engineer has regular 1-1s and a written growth plan within your first 90 days.
- Performance problems get named and acted on within weeks with a written expectation and a date.
- Client engineering leaders name you, not the founder, as their technical point of contact.
- Scope misses shrink. Proposals get a technical gut-check before they go out, and estimates hold.
- You stay at about 50% billable through year one.
- Engineers stay, grow, and say the team is better run than it was.
