We're looking for a part-time contractor to **own the administrative backbone of our business.** You're great at taking an assigned task, figuring out the details and steps, and getting it done. You'll work directly with our leadership team and touch everything from client invoicing to intern onboarding.

Hours are flexible and will vary month to month, but generally land around **10–20 hours per month.**

## What You'll Do

- **Client invoicing.** Generate and send invoices accurately and on time in QuickBooks Online, track payment status, and follow up when needed.
- **Payroll and contractor payments.** Process payroll and make sure contractors are paid correctly each cycle, via Gusto.
- **Employee onboarding and offboarding.** Coordinate the logistics of bringing new team members on and transitioning people out smoothly.
- **Internal organization.** Keep our internal systems tidy and up to date, and help with general operational tasks as they come up.

## What You Bring

### Must-Haves

- **U.S.-based only.**
- Strong attention to detail. You catch things others miss.
- Familiarity with **AI tools** (ChatGPT, Claude, etc.) and comfort using them in your workflow.
- A self-sufficient work style. You don't need a lot of hand-holding to get moving.
- Experience with **Notion** for documentation and project tracking.
- Experience with **Slack** for internal communications.
- Solid experience with **QuickBooks Online (QBO) and Gusto**.
- Clear, professional communication.

### Nice-to-Haves

- Comfortable working in **Google Drive and Google Sheets**.
- Exposure to **SaaS tools** broadly, or a proven ability to pick up new software quickly.

## Why Masterpoint?

- **Competitive rate.** The hourly rate for this role is **$50/hour**.
- **Remote day-to-day.** We've been remote since 2016, with most of our team in North America and one teammate in Ukraine. Some tasks, like sending out the swag packages we keep in Boulder, go better with occasional in-person time with our CEO, Matt, so being in the Boulder, CO area is preferred.
- **We're an organization that cares**, and that means your work helps us support meaningful causes:
  - We're a [1% for the Planet](https://www.onepercentfortheplanet.org/) member and give 1% of our gross income towards organizations supporting our environment.
  - We [give an additional 1% of our gross income towards the support of Ukraine](/blog/supporting-ukraine/) due to the unjust invasion of their country.
  - We support Open Source. Through our own open source projects, maintenance of external open source libraries, and donations, we're making sure to give back to the open source ecosystem that we all rely on and ❤️ so much.
